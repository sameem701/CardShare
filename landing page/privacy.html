Waitlist privacy

This policy covers the CardShare landing page and early-access waitlist.

What we collect and why

When you opt in, CardShare stores your email address and the time of your signup. We use these details to manage the waitlist and email you about CardShare’s launch.

How we store your signup

Google Sheets stores the waitlist. CardShare limits access to people who manage early access.

How long we keep it

We keep your signup while the waitlist is active and delete it when launch communications are complete. You can ask us to remove it sooner.

Your choices

To withdraw consent or request deletion, message CardShare through the official channel that shared the signup link.

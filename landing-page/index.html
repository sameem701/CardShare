A few things to know.

Do I need my own bank card?

No. You can ask an eligible cardholder in your trusted circle to place an online order for you, when the offer terms allow it.

How much does the cardholder earn?

The requester and cardholder agree on how to split any savings before placing the order. The cardholder’s earnings depend on that agreement and the savings achieved.

Does anyone else see my card details?

No payment credentials should be shared. CardShare is designed around bank and card type information; the cardholder completes payment with their own card.

How are payments handled?

Agree on the order and reimbursement with the person you trust before purchasing. CardShare does not hold funds in escrow or guarantee repayment.

Are savings guaranteed?

No. Savings depend on the purchase and applicable bank and merchant terms. Only proceed when those terms allow the arrangement.

When can I start using CardShare?

CardShare is preparing for launch in Pakistan. There is no confirmed public launch date yet.
